ABOUT YOUR MT5 ACCOUNT

How MT5 fits in

Your MetaTrader 5 Android app account cannot be connected directly to this browser page. MT5's official Python connection requires the desktop terminal on a Windows computer. This mobile version uses TradingView charts instead. To display your broker's exact MT5 prices later, a secure bridge running on a computer/server would be needed.
